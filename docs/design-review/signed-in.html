A One watch shows up as four entries P1

  • The feed also shows "Liked @glory42's review" to glory42, which is your own like on your own review. Hide self-likes from the feed.
  • Fix: when building the feed and recent activity, group activities by (user, media) within a short window (for example, the same day). Show one card that combines everything: rating, liked, rewatch and a review excerpt. Grouping can happen entirely when the feed is read, so the activities table doesn't need to change.

C Signed-in actions and rating P2

  • The rating control is a vertical rocket slider, 160px tall, with a Save button. It takes up as much room as the poster, and the extra Save step makes rating slower. It's a fun idea, but it doesn't belong in the sidebar.
  • Log still says "Log" after you've watched the film. Show your last watch date ("Watched Nov 2") and change the button to "Log again".
  • Small fixes: "1 logs" should read "1 log", and "+ watchlist" is lowercase while the other buttons are uppercase.
  • Fix: keep the rocket and the 1–10 scale; they're what makes Interis different from star-based apps. Turn the launch track sideways: a 0–10 horizontal track (still in 0.5 steps) where the rocket flies to your score and leaves a glowing exhaust trail. It saves when you let go, so the Save button goes. SpaceRatingInput already does this with autoSave, which commits on pointer-up rather than on every drag frame. Tapping the same value again still clears the rating.
  • Keep the row of 10 rockets (SpaceRatingDisplay) for showing ratings elsewhere (reviews, feed, grouped activity). Rocket ratings then look the same everywhere.

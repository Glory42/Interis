The reel / slate style, applied across the app

These pages use the same rules as the archive hero you approved. Six rules keep everything consistent:

Slate barsBlack strips with mono metadata at the top and bottom of an image: format, week, runtime.
Credits, not icon cardsFacts and cast written as end credits, with dotted leaders between label and value.
Rockets everywhereThe 1–10 rocket rating everywhere, including the season and episode ratings that currently use stars.
Reel ticks for progressOne small cell per episode, so you can see progress at a glance.
Specific copyWeek numbers, dates and real counts. Never "Unknown", never placeholder text.
Nearly square corners2–4px corners and hairline borders instead of rounded cards with glows.

1 · Movie and TV detail pages

  • The backdrop sits inside a letterbox with slate bars, and stays in full colour because it's the film's own art. The poster overlaps the bottom of the frame.
  • The title reads like an opening credit: a director kicker, the title, then the tagline (the API already returns it).
  • The 6-card facts grid (with icons and "Unknown") and the cast chips become one credits block. Missing values simply don't appear, so TV pages never show "created by Unknown".

2 · TV seasons and episodes

  • Season row = one reel. One cell per episode, filled when watched. It replaces the generic "WATCHED" pill, and even the 366-episode season shows its progress in a single glance.
  • Episode rows read like a call sheet: E01 · title · runtime · air date, with one tick on the left to mark it watched. Like, rating and review only appear on hover or focus, so 400 rows don't each repeat four buttons. The episode description opens on click.
  • Mark up to here on hover marks every earlier episode as watched, which turns catching up on long anime into one click.
  • The RatingSelect star dropdown becomes a small rocket rating, so the rocket stays consistent everywhere.
  • The sidebar's "Ratings (S/E): 0 / Likes (S/E): 0" becomes one reel counter: 416/416 · COMPLETED NOV 2.

3 · Feed

  • Day dividers become slate lines (MON · NOV 2 · WK 45) instead of small grey dates.
  • One watch becomes one grouped card (from the signed-in review). Small events from the same person collapse into a single line: "liked 2 titles" with tiny posters.
  • The ALL / MOVIE / SERIAL tabs become the same filter sentence as the archive.
  • The right rail becomes Now showing with outlined rank numbers, plus Your week with real numbers, so the feed doesn't look empty when the people you follow are quiet.
  • Times and "Your week" numbers in this mockup are illustrative, not your real stats.

Person pages: actors and directors

One layout for /actor/$slug and /director/$slug, with role-aware labels. The page is built like an end-credits roll: slate, portrait, facts with leaders, then a dated filmography you can filter with a sentence.

What's wrong now

  • Placeholder copy: "Unknown" birthday and place (PersonDetailHeader.tsx:41,49), "Died -" for the living (:45), aliases shown as a bare count (:53), "No biography is available…" (:58), "Year unknown" (PersonCreditsGrid.tsx:66), and placehold.co "No Poster" images (helpers.ts:7,15).
  • Developer data: TMDB ID and Popularity in the sidebar (PersonDetailSidebar.tsx:42,51). The pills at Header:14 show route hints, not the jobs the person did.
  • Off-language: the name and titles are mono (Header:29, Grid:60). There are rounded-xl/rounded-full cards and pills (Grid:44, Tabs:35,56), a glass breadcrumb (PersonDetailPage.tsx:97), and Lucide icons on every card (Grid:69,73).
  • Broken breadcrumb: "Directors" links to /serials and "Actors" to /movies (Page:101–106). styles.ts:4 gives directors the serial accent.
  • Crew is one pile: Director sits next to Key Animation (types.ts:10). The dedupe key includes job (people-credit-normalization.service.ts:87–93), so Godzilla Minus One appears once per job. "Combined (n)" counts those duplicates (Page:79–83).
  • Credits silently cut: credits are sorted newest-first and then sliced to 120 combined and 100 per medium (normalization.service.ts:18–19,197). Kamiki has 76 films and 75 TV credits, so the default view drops his earliest work. Undated credits sink to the end (:61–66), and upcoming titles look the same as released ones.
  • No hierarchy: no known-for section, no year grouping, and up to 120 vote-count cards rendered at once with no paging or memo (Grid:36,85–91). episodeCount and the 24 images.profiles are fetched but never used.
  • Biography: one full-length <p> (Header:57). Line breaks collapse, and the trailing Wikipedia/CC-BY-SA line reads as body text.
  • Backend: six uncached TMDB calls per view. Details is awaited before the other five (people-detail.service.ts:31,36), movie and TV credits duplicate the combined call, and the response is sent with no-store (people.controller.ts:34).

1 · Director, desktop (default theme)

  • Slate carries role and life facts. The duotone backdrop (grayscale, movie tint, grain) comes from the top known-for credit's backdropPath and is captioned as a still. Minus Zero is used because it's the only backdrop in the sample.
  • The portrait is a tall 2:3 frame overlapping the backdrop. Facts and links below it are credits with leaders, and empty rows are dropped.
  • The kicker shows the top three jobs and the stat line real counts. The bio is cut to three lines, with the Wikipedia licence as a footnote.
  • Known for: outlined ranks by TMDB vote count. The order here is illustrative because the sample has no vote counts.
  • Filmography: job tabs with counts, then the filter sentence, then decades. There's one row per title with its other jobs merged in, upcoming titles get a badge, and a title without a poster is typeset.

Needs backend work

  • Page, don't truncate: drop the 120/100 caps, return total, and accept job/media/order/limit=40/offset. Add knownFor (top 8 by voteCount, with backdrops).
  • Cache TMDB with createCachedTmdbFetcher keyed by person id. Run one Promise.all and derive movie/TV credits from the combined call.
  • Batch viewer status: one inArray query for watched, rating and watchlist over the visible credit ids. It powers "seen 6 of 25", the row marks and the "Your rating" order.
  • Biography source: split off the Wikipedia/CC-BY-SA trailer into biographySource. isVoice and isUpcoming can be derived on the client.

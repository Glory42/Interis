The deck

What this proves: the pacing, the one-idea-per-card rhythm, the tap-through, and the persona payoff. The four most developed cards are the cover, volume, time machine and persona; the rest show the deck's range.

Read: the design spec for the data contract, persona scoring, motion timings, share cards and accessibility notes.

The full deck

13 cards · 5 acts

The playable prototype above uses this order. Conditional cards drop out when the data is missing; a thin year (<10 logs) becomes a five-card short, and an empty year becomes a single quiet card.

The persona

Behavioural, deterministic, and positive or neutral. The highest score wins; below 0.45 the result is The Devotee. Say “This year, you were…”, and show the receipts on the card.

Motion & variants

  • Enter500ms fade + 14px rise, 60–80ms stagger, ease-out.
  • Count900ms count-up; instant with reduced motion.
  • Bars700ms scaleY, 40–50ms stagger.
  • Cards300ms crossfade + 1.02→1.0 scale. No horizontal slide.
  • Advance8s per card; cover waits for Begin; tap right/left.
  • Pausefocus, hidden tab, or the pause button. No hover pause.
  • Reducedmanual only, no count-ups or Ken Burns; 150ms fades.

Thin year: cover, volume, titles, one highlight, end credits. No persona below 5 logs.

Empty year: one quiet card + “Log a film”. No share.

Partial year: “2025 so far” on the cover and volume; the end card says the year isn't over.

10 · Phone browser (390px)

  • 8 tabs = a scrollable text strip. Today phones get 8 icons with no labels (ProfileTabs.tsx:138), and Likes, Watchlist and Lists are hard to tell apart. A tab sheet would take 2 taps and hide where you are. The strip keeps labels, scrolls the active tab into view, fades at the edge, and sticks under the top bar on scroll. It's the familiar mobile-web pattern (GitHub, YouTube).
  • Row ⋯ (44px tap area) opens a bottom sheet, and delete confirms inside it. Review and list detail get a sticky bottom bar (♡ Like · Reply · ⋯) and a 16px reply input.
